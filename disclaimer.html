Craftsmanship & Alpine Safety Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Storm Parka Vale, Manhattan Atelier

1. General Alpine Notice

The mountaineering masterclasses, thermal insulation treatises, hydrostatic membrane calculations, and alpine commentary published across the Storm Parka Vale digital platforms and at our flagship salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, are presented exclusively for general educational, historical, and technical appreciation. While our master tailors and alpine textile engineers make every reasonable effort to ensure that all published technical guidance reflects verified high-altitude physics, Storm Parka Vale makes no guarantee against extreme meteorological acts of nature.

2. Thermal Scope & Individual Physiological Variations

Our outerwear is laboratory-tested under standardized thermal manikin protocols (EN 342 / ISO 15831). However, individual perceived warmth and cold tolerance vary significantly based on user metabolic rate, exhaustion levels, hydration, altitude acclimatization, and accompanying layering gear. Explorers acknowledge that high-altitude mountaineering operates within hazardous physical environments and that proper expedition planning, acclimatization pacing, and certified guide accompaniment remain the explorer's personal responsibility.

3. Garment Maintenance & DWR Membrane Care

While our technical membranes deliver 35,000mm hydrostatic head waterproof protection, outdoor dirt, campfire smoke, and body oils naturally accumulate on fabric surfaces over time. Patrons must follow our certified technical down laundering and Durable Water Repellent (DWR) heat-reactivation guidelines to maintain optimal water-beading performance across multi-year polar expeditions.

4. Bespoke Color Representation & Textile Nuances

Our digital photography and interactive configurator are calibrated to reflect the deep storm charcoal, glacial blue slate, and aurora cyan textures of our technical outerwear with high fidelity. However, variations in individual monitor calibrations and ambient salon lighting may cause subtle tonal differences between digital screen previews and physical hand-tailored technical fabrics. Patrons are invited to inspect hand-stitched membrane and down samples at our Mercer Street salon prior to placing bespoke orders.

5. Scientific References & Polar Expedition Studies

Our editorial journal frequently cites historical polar expedition archives, glaciology research papers, and textile engineering studies to provide scientific context for our patrons. These scholarly citations are curated solely to enhance your alpine appreciation and technical understanding. Storm Parka Vale exercises no administrative authority, editorial control, or technical oversight over third-party alpine institutes or referenced academic laboratories, and we assume no legal liability for external research publications.

6. Limitation of Atelier Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable statutory law, Storm Parka Vale, its alpine tailors, executives, and affiliates shall not be held liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, or high-altitude injury resulting from improper mountain route selection, extreme avalanches, or unsuitable expedition conditions. Our total aggregate liability concerning any verified commercial dispute or craftsmanship claim shall be strictly limited to the verified purchase price paid by the explorer for the specific outerwear garment.
