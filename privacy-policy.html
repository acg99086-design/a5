Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Storm Parka Vale, Manhattan Atelier

1. Introduction & Alpine Privacy Ethos

At Storm Parka Vale, operating our alpine outerwear atelier and sub-zero testing facility at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold explorer confidentiality and personal privacy to the highest standard of craftsmanship. We recognize that commissioning bespoke alpine parkas, scheduling cold chamber trials, or ordering specialized high-altitude expedition systems involves sharing sensitive personal records and body tailoring dimensions. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact protocols we employ to collect, process, safeguard, and retain your data across our digital portals and Manhattan salon.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our atelier, we collect essential client information including your full name, expedition destination, email address, telephone number, billing records, and bespoke morphological sizing measurements recorded during private fittings. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive outerwear configurator or browse our alpine research treatises, our web servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths via Google Tag manager. These technical metrics enable our digital artisans to optimize browsing performance and protect transaction security across international luxury markets.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate outerwear manufacturing and commercial operations, including fulfilling custom storm parka commissions, coordinating cold room fitting sessions, scheduling personalized tailoring consultations, generating formal invoices, and delivering expedition updates. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to closed-door outerwear unveils and seasonal alpine releases. We maintain an absolute policy never to sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute your personal information or expedition records to any third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital commission archives, client garment patterns, and billing information entrusted to Storm Parka Vale are stored on private cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and strict administrative access controls. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors, ensuring our atelier never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Storm Parka Vale extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to profile records, restrict processing activities, or demand the permanent deletion of your client records from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our administrative desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain client fitting archives and bespoke pattern registries for as long as your bespoke profile remains active, enabling seamless repeat orders, custom repair servicing, and longitudinal expedition sizing adjustments without requiring repetitive intake sessions. If you choose to conclude your patronage or request data expungement, all electronic records and custom garment drafts are securely purged and permanently deleted within thirty business days, subject only to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements mandated by United States commercial law.

7. Atelier Contact for Privacy Governance

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our leadership team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Storm Parka Vale, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our compliance team will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard business timelines.
